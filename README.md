## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. 
She wants to understand her business performance using 
two weeks of daily sales records and current stock levels. 
The analysis focuses on identifying sales trends, comparing 
weekday and weekend performance, classifying daily sales, 
identifying products that need restocking and calculating 
gross profit margins. The goal is to provide practical 
recommendations to improve business performance and stock 
management.

## How to Run

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales over 14 days were KES 46,450, with 
   Week 2 recording 10.66% growth.

2. Weekend average sales were KES 4,900, which was 
   82.50% higher than weekday average sales.

3. Tomatoes and Sukuma require immediate restocking 
   because their quantities are below reorder levels.
